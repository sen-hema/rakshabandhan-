A Letter, From Wherever I Am

My love,

Every Raksha Bandhan I've tied that thread on your wrist and made you promise to still let me boss you around even when we're both old. This year the distance won that fight, and it's the one thing about being here that I haven't made peace with.

But a thread was never really about the thread. It was always the promise underneath it, and that part doesn't need a flight home to be true. So here it is, in writing, so you can hold me to it forever: in this life and in every life after it, I am choosing to be your elder sister. I will find you, annoy you, protect you, and pick you again and again. You don't get to swap me out for a cooler sister in your next life. This is a lifelong, multi-lifetime contract, and there are no loopholes.

Until I can tie it on your wrist myself, consider this thread tied anyway, somewhere it can't fray.
